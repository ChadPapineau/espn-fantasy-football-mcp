## What "verified" means

A family, as a league's scoring settings use it, is **verified** when both hold
(`src/domain/scoring/verification.ts`, `familyVerification`):

1. **Derivation evidenced** (`FAMILY_EVIDENCE`): the way a derived line (nflverse, a projection
   sample) gets its member values from the scalar — `bracketize`, or the pbp long-TD counts — is
   reproduced on ESPN's own raw values on every recorded line.
2. **Golden-covered members** (`GOLDEN_COVERED_STAT_IDS`): every member the league scores (non-zero
   points for the member's class) is scored by a recorded league whose box scores carry a
   **non-zero** ESPN `appliedStats` value for it (actual or projected line), which the recorded
   golden compares per stat (≤ 0.005). A member only ever compared at 0 does not count: an engine
   that dropped the rule would pass that comparison.

A member the league carries at zero points is ignored: it cannot mis-score. `espn_get_league` is to
report the result per family (`families[].verified`, `unverified_stat_ids`) — the A1 owner wires it.

## Results

- **`long_td_bonus` — verified.** League-c scores all six ids (15, 16, 35, 36, 45, 46); the golden
  compares 33 non-zero actual values and every projected one. ESPN's ids are cumulative (a 50+ yard
  TD sets the 40+ id too). Phase 2 closes the nflverse gap of plan 08 §4.3: `longTdCounts` over the
  ds_pbp scoring plays reproduces ESPN's raw ids on every recorded QB/RB/WR/TE week, so a derived
  line that carries them is `complete` (weekly totals alone stay underivable).
- **`per_n_yards` — verified** for the divisors league-c scores (8 = every 25 passing yards, 28 =
  every 10 rushing, 48 = every 10 receiving). Every per-N value ESPN sends, for every divisor and on
  actual and projected lines alike, is `max(0, ⌊scalar / N⌋)` (15,434 values; ESPN omits the id when
  the scalar is negative). The other 23 divisors share that derivation but no recorded league scores
  them, so they stay `verified: false` (D8).
- **`fg_distance` member 74 (`fg_50p`, the legacy 50+) — not verified (D8).** No recorded league
  scores stat 74, so ESPN's points for it were never observed. ESPN's raw 74 equals 198 + 201 (made
  50–59 plus 60+) on every recorded kicker week, so the bounds `[50, ∞)` are as the registry has
  them; a league that scores 74 is reported `fg_distance: verified: false, unverified_stat_ids:
  ["74"]` until one is recorded. The rest of the family (80, 77, 198, 201) is verified on
  league-b and league-c.
- **Also not verified (D8):** every FG-attempt bucket (no recorded league scores attempts), the
  missed buckets 76, 200, 203, the PA tier 121 and the YA tiers 131–132 (no recorded league scores
  them), and the HC margin items (no head-coach line is recorded; league-a lists 161–166 with the HC
  override at 0). League-a scores the YA tier 128 (under 100 yards), but no recorded game or
  projection ever triggered it, so league-a's `dst_yards_allowed` is reported unverified on 128.
- **`yardage_bonus` — open on plan 08 A-2.** The 300–399 / 100–199 indicators fire exactly at their
  lower bound on every recorded line, but no recorded game reached 400 passing or 200 rushing or
  receiving yards, so whether 17, 37 and 56 also pay above their upper bound (the engine's
  cumulative reading) is unobserved. A test fails as soon as a re-recording holds such a game.
- **The D/ST points-allowed derivation (plan 08 U-6) — pinned, 70/70.** ESPN's stat 120 is the
  opponent's final score net of its defence's interception- and fumble-return TDs against this
  team's offence (6 each — the try after them still counts) and of its safeties (2 each, a penalty
  safety included); kick- and punt-return TDs are not netted. Seven of the 70 recorded D/ST weeks
  discriminate (below): three INT-return TDs and two fumble-return TDs against the offence, one
  safety and one punt-return TD. `final_score` is wrong on six of them. Unobserved, so still the
  [U] reading: a kickoff-return or blocked-kick TD against, a defensive conversion return, and a
  net below zero (floored at 0).

Notes on the sources:

- `pointsAllowedFromTeamWeek` reads the **opponent's** `ds_stats_team_week` row: `def_tds`
  (interception returns), `min(fumble_recovery_tds, fumble_recovery_opp)` (fumble returns) and
  `def_safeties`. Over 2024–2026 (1,268 team-games) `def_tds` and `def_safeties` equal the pbp
  counts on every team-game; the `min()` reading over-counts fumble-return TDs on 5 team-games
  (an offensive own-recovery TD in a game where the defence also recovered a fumble), so when the
  game's ds_pbp rows are given the TD counts come from them instead.
- The Phase-1 path (`ds_team_defense_week` from player rows) reproduces 68 of the 70 weeks: a
  penalty safety has no player or team row, and one team-level row carries a safety pbp does not
  show. Neither changes a tier on the recorded weeks.
- `ds_pbp`'s row filter drops `no_play` rows, and the one penalty safety sits on one: safeties from
  the store's pbp alone miss that week.
