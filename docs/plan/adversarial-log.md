### 1.2 Details

#### OBJ-01 — The 1a engine gate can be satisfied by fixtures the engine itself scored — **blocking**

- **Target.** Plan 10 §3.1a *Fixtures* ("`fx-10h` and its variants … generated from rules or recorded from a public league keyless and scrubbed"), A1a ("the golden test matches `appliedTotal` within 0.01 and every `appliedStats[statId]` within 0.005 for every rostered player-week of ≥ 3 anonymised `mBoxscore` weeks"), A-1; plan 09 A-2 and §4 (`scripts/gen-fixtures.ts` — "fx-10h + variants from rules (never from a real league in CI)"); research 06 A-6; plan 05 §3.1 (recording is manual and credentialed or public); plan 08 §6 step 1 and step 6.
- **Claim.** Three facts collide. (1) The engine's only external truth is ESPN's per-stat `appliedStats` and `appliedTotal` (plan 08 E8: "the ESPN line is the golden source"). (2) `fx-10h` is the reference format — 10 teams, half-PPR, 5-pt passing TD, priority waivers, 5 BE / 2 IR — and the only ESPN league with that `S` is Chad's private league, which is Phase 1b; the public probe league is FAAB, non-PPR, 4-pt TD (03 §B.1). (3) Plans 09 and 10 allow `fx-10h` to be *generated from rules*. A generated `mBoxscore` gets its `appliedStats` from whatever scorer `gen-fixtures.ts` uses — `stat_map.ts` or a hand copy of it — so A1a's "per stat ≤ 0.005" compares the engine with itself. The 1a exit gate can be green with a wrong id row, the wrong 103/104 orientation, a wrong bracket bound or a missed override, and the first real check is A1b — gated on D0. Under D0-not-accepted the plan's own text (plan 10 §5 D0: "Phase 1a ships as a fixture-and-nflverse product … and never touches Chad's league") ships the engine unvalidated forever. Plan 08 §6 step 6 half-admits the limit ("It proves the engine reproduces ESPN *for the settings in the fixtures*") without noticing that the fixtures may not be ESPN's.
- **Evidence.** 03 P07: `mBoxscore&scoringPeriodId=4` returned 200 (325 KB) with **no cookies** on the public probe league; 04 §B.1.6: `statsOfficial: true` for all 48 games of weeks 1–3 — three final weeks of real `appliedStats` are recordable keyless today. 03 §B.1 for the probe league's format. Plan 10 A-1 and plan 09 A-2 for the "generated" branch.
- **Severity.** Blocking — every P0 number (E1's `own` half, E2's assignment, E5's `opportunity_cost`, A5's `match`, the K/D-ST brackets) is scored by this engine, and a correctness gate that can be satisfied vacuously is not a gate. It is a cheap blocking: the fix is a sentence and a recording.
- **What would satisfy me.** (a) A1a requires ≥ 3 **recorded** `mBoxscore` weeks (anonymised per 03 §F.3) from a real league — the public probe league keyless (D2) in 1a, Chad's league in 1b — and forbids generated `appliedStats`: `gen-fixtures.ts` may derive variants only by mutating recorded bodies' non-scoring fields (names, statuses, slots, ownership) and must never write `appliedStats` or `appliedTotal` (a test asserts every fixture's scoring fields hash to a recorded original). (b) The reference-format assertions (item 53 at 0.5, item 4 at 5, −2/−2, the K/D-ST families present in `S`) become property/unit tests over `normalizeSettings` on a hand-written `mSettings` in 1a; the reference-format golden is A1b. (c) Plan 08 §6 step 6 and plan 10 §5 D0 state that under D0-not-accepted the engine is validated only on the probe league's `S`, and list which families that leaves `verified: false`.

#### OBJ-02 — v1's ensemble weight is unsupported by the evidence it cites — significant

- **Target.** Plan 07 E1: "v1 = ESPN mean + trailing nflverse lines through the engine with the positional CVs"; `inputs: { espn, own, weight_espn }`; plan 01 D15 ("a first-class, labelled input to an ensemble, never the sole basis of a number we call ours"); plan 10 A11a (soft, reported) and B14 (Phase 2: "if ESPN alone wins under this scoring, `weight_espn` moves toward 1"); research 04 §B.1.1 step 3 ("default to the simple average until eight weeks of data exist").
- **Claim.** The only accuracy evidence in the research is FFA's: "The FFA Average, a simple average of all available projection sources, outperformed individual sources in 63% of head-to-head comparisons" — an average of nine *expert* weekly sources whose MAEs sit within ~5 % of each other (04 §B.1.1). v1's "own" half is not an expert source; it is a trailing-window stat line scored under `S` — the trailing-4 that research 05 §8.4 #2 lists as baseline **(a)** *to beat*, and that sib §1's whole evaluation exists to beat. Averaging a mid-pack expert with a naive baseline at equal weight is not what the 63 % result says, and on the plan's own framing it will more likely raise MAE than lower it. Every P0 decision — E2 lineups, E5's `opportunity_cost`, K/D-ST value — then runs on a number that is worse than the free one and labelled "ours" (`meta.estimate: true`). A11a compares `objective: mean` regret against the "start by ESPN's projection" baseline **softly**, so `v0.1.0` can tag while the product loses to its own input. D15's principle is being satisfied by degrading the number, not by improving it.
- **Evidence.** 04 §B.1.1 (the quoted sentence and the source list); 05 §8.4 #2 ("scored under `S` against (a) trailing-4, (b) season-to-date and … (c) ESPN's native projection"); plan 10 A11a ("soft, both numbers reported"); plan 07 E1's degradation row ("nflverse stale → ESPN-anchored with the positional CV") — which is the better default, offered only as a fallback.
- **Severity.** Significant — methodology overclaim on the MVP's central number; the brief's dimension 5 asks exactly this.
- **What would satisfy me.** (a) v1 ships `weight_espn = 1.0` by default: ESPN's mean is the point estimate; the trailing line contributes the distribution shape (`position_cv` σ, turnover left tail) and the `disagreement` flag only. (b) The weight moves below 1 only when a replay over the recorded fixture weeks (1a) or B14's prospective backtest shows the mixture beats ESPN alone on MAE/CRPS *under `S`*; A11a's ESPN-baseline comparison becomes the gating rule for the shipped default ("if `v1-ensemble` regret > ESPN-alone regret on the fixture weeks, `weight_espn` ships at 1.0"). (c) `meta.estimate: true`, `data.inputs[]` and the label stay exactly as designed — the label is right; the weight is what changes.

#### OBJ-03 — The priority premium is a point estimate with a ×2 band the tools hide — significant

- **Target.** Plan 07 E5 (`premium: number`, `premium_basis: "cold_start_table" | "league_fitted"`, `verdict: "claim" | "pass" | …`), C11; plan 09 §3.8 output contract ("`Π(k, W)` with `premium_basis`; per candidate … `claim | pass | fa_add_after_run`"); plan 10 A9a ("`Π(2, 13)` within 0.5 of 05 §1.2's table (29.5) … `claim ⇔ s ≥ Π` holds for every candidate"); plan 07 A-6 (the cold-start `P(role holds)` prior).
- **Claim.** Research 05 §1.2's own sensitivity analysis: surplus rates ×0.5 / ×1.5 move `Π(1,9)` from 25.0 to **12.1 / 42.8** and `Π(5,9)` from 9.7 to 5.3 / 12.1 — a factor of two either way from "the parameter to learn first", which at P0 is a cold-start guess. The P0 surplus is `Σ_w P(role holds) × max(0, proj_espn − opportunity_cost)` with `P(role holds)` from a hand-set prior (A-6) that §1.2 calls "the single most sensitive input". So at P0 both sides of `s ≥ Π` are hand-set, and the acceptance criterion tests that the code reproduces the research table — a regression on the DP, not evidence about any league. The Skill then prints `claim | pass` as a verdict: a candidate with `s = 20` against `Π(2,13) = 29.5` is a crisp "pass" that becomes a "claim" at the ×0.5 end of the band. The sibling's `coin_flip` discipline (adopted here for lineups via `Dist.basis`) was not applied to claims, where the format's distinguishing decision lives.
- **Evidence.** 05 §1.2 (table, sensitivities, the sentence on what to learn first); 05 §1.6 example A (a flip driven by `P(role holds)`); plan 07 E5 output shape; plan 10 A9a.
- **Severity.** Significant — false precision on the MVP's flagship feature, with an acceptance test that cannot catch it.
- **What would satisfy me.** (a) E5 returns `premium_band: { low, high }` from re-solving the DP at ×0.5 and ×1.5 surplus (two extra solves of a 10 × 17 table — trivially cheap) and `verdict: "marginal"` when `s` falls inside the band. (b) `waivers` prints the band and calls marginal claims marginal. (c) A9a adds "on `fx-10h` the band is populated and every verdict inside it is `marginal`" (hard) and keeps the 29.5 reproduction as the regression it is. (d) `premium_basis: cold_start_table` stays until the league's own feed re-solves Π (C5).

#### OBJ-05 — The launchd keychain fallback creates two credential stores — significant

- **Target.** Plan 06 §2 ("Keychain under launchd: … whether the keychain read prompts (and where the prompt appears) is **[A-2]** … If it prompts, the credentialed jobs use the file store (`EFF_CREDENTIAL_STORE=file` for the jobs only)"); plan 02 §2.2 (one store, selected by `EFF_CREDENTIAL_STORE`; "read-only after setup"; the server reloads on a `storedAt` change), §8 #1/#7; plan 03 §6 (reload compares "the store's `storedAt` (keychain `meta` / file mtime)"), §8 (`uninstall` deletes "the keychain items … and/or `session.json`"); plan 02 A-1 ("same node binary + addon → one ACL entry").
- **Claim.** The fallback quietly turns one store into two. `eff setup` writes to the store its env selects, so for the jobs to read a file it must write both (unspecified); `--reset` and `uninstall` must clear both (plan 03 §8 says "and/or"); the server's reload compares `storedAt` against one; `doctor` #6/#7 check one; and the at-rest exposure the keychain was chosen to reduce — plan 01 D12: "at-rest protection is worth a native addon here" — is now doubled, because the plaintext file exists *precisely because* the keychain is in use. Plan 02 §8 has no row for the two-store state. The fallback is not unlikely to fire: plan 06 §2 itself says a launchd-started job "runs as the user but without a GUI session context", and A-1's one-ACL-entry claim is an assumption (Keychain ACLs are per executable; whether a non-GUI launch of the same binary is prompt-free is platform behaviour I have not re-verified and the plan marks [U] in 03 §G.1 #14).
- **Evidence.** The quoted plan 06 §2 sentence; plan 02 §2.2 "Multiple processes" row (no rotation → no lock — true, but assumes one store); plan 03 §8 step 2.
- **Severity.** Significant — a security design with an undefined state in its credential store, reachable through the plan's own fallback.
- **What would satisfy me.** One store per install, decided at `eff setup` time: (a) `eff setup` runs a launchd-context test (a one-shot agent that reads the `meta` item) and, if that read prompts or fails, records `EFF_CREDENTIAL_STORE=file` in `config.json` for the **whole** install and says why; or (b) the jobs read the *same* keychain item through the `security`-CLI variant (plan 02 §7.2's fallback) — never a second copy. (c) Plan 02 §8 gains a row making the two-store state forbidden, and `doctor` #6 fails when both the `meta` item and `session.json` exist. (d) Plan 03 §8 `uninstall` says "both, always".

- **Evidence.** Plan 03 §3's key list; plan 02 §7.1 host allow-list row; plan 01 §5.4 hard limits; plan 09 §2 routing text; 03 §F.1 cadence and §D.2 for the 2024 timeline.
