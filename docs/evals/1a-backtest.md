## Start/sit (A11a)

Each recorded team-week is replayed at `objective: mean` with E1 `v1-ensemble` distributions. Regret
is the hindsight-best lineup's realised points minus the rule's, summed over the team-weeks; the
manager's lineup is what each team actually started. Week 1 has no "last week" in the recordings.

- **ESPN-projection baseline: not informative in v1.** At `weight_espn = 1.0` (ADV OBJ-02) the
  `mean` lineup *is* the ESPN-projection lineup, so the two regret columns are identical by
  construction (plan 07 E13; R2 nit 2). The gating rule holds: `weight_espn` ships at 1.0.
- **Last week's points** is the comparison that carries information here (soft: `mean` regret ≤ it).
- Three weeks of nine to twelve teams are a small sample: these numbers describe the recordings and
  prove nothing about a season.

## K and D/ST (A12a)

Every rostered K and D/ST of each league-week is a candidate (free agents are not in the box
scores). Each column is Spearman's ρ between the rule's ranking and the realised ESPN points, per
league-week; the bracket model is `kdstExpectation` under that league's own scoring settings with
implied totals from the nflverse lines (home = (total + spread) / 2).

- **The bracket model and the implied-total baseline rank identically by construction.** With one
  input per candidate (the opponent's implied total for a D/ST, the own team's for a K) the model's
  expectation is monotone in it, so the two ρ columns agree to the digit; the model's value is in
  the *magnitude* — points under this league's own tiers, the hold-vs-stream margin — not the order.
  Separating them needs a second input (weather, a defence's own rates), P1 work.
- Kicker ρ is negative on most of these weeks — three weeks of 8–20 kickers is noise-dominated (a
  missed 50-yarder moves a kicker's week by 8 points). Report, never tune, on this sample.

## Waivers — the hindsight replay of research 05 §1.6 (A9a)

Plan 10 A9a's soft part: research 05 §8.4 #4's replay over the nflverse 2024–2025 player-week
excerpt — weeks 2–14 of each season, a 10-team league — comparing the realised surplus per claim of
the shipped rule against "claim the top trending player" and "always claim the top hindsight player
from your slot". Generated by `tests/backtest/waivers.test.ts`, which fails when this section differs
from the run; the A9a hard parts are `tests/domain/analytics/waiver-dp.test.ts`, `waivers.test.ts` and
the end-to-end `tests/e2e/acceptance.test.ts`.

- **Available** at a claim week: a QB/RB/WR/TE whose points per game over the earlier weeks rank
  below QB14 / RB36 / WR38 / TE14 (research 05 §1.6, ≈ the rostered counts), or who has not played.
- **Realised surplus** of a claim at week t: `Σ_{w=t}^{17} max(0, pts_w − replacement)` under the
  reference league's scoring, with research 05 §4.1's per-game replacement levels for that season
  (the drop is replacement level). Points come from the shipped translator and engine under
  `fx-10h`'s settings (half-PPR, 5-point passing TDs, −2 interceptions and fumbles lost).
- **The rule** sees a forecast, not the hindsight: the player's last ≤ 4 games' points per game over
  replacement, held for the weeks left with the shipped P(role holds) prior; it claims, best first and
  at most five per run, every player whose forecast clears the shipped cold-start premium
  `Π(k, W)` (N = 10).
- **Trending** (nflverse has no adds feed): the available player with the most points last week — the
  box-score chase a trending list follows. **Hindsight** claims the best realised surplus available:
  a ceiling, not a strategy.
- **Simulated rivals** (research 05 §1.2's cold start): a claim at position k wins when none of the
  k − 1 teams ahead claims the player, `(1 − q)^(k−1)` with the DP's demand curve `q(r)`; a win sends
  the team to N; a run without a win moves it up by `Binomial(k − 1, 0.25)`; a lost player is gone.
  Every starting position 1–10, 200 seeded paths each.
