## C7 — K/D-ST on history

**Test:** `tests/backtest/kdst-history.test.ts`. **Data:** `tests/backtest/data/kdst_history`
(nflverse 2023–2025, CC-BY 4.0; every regular-season game of weeks 1–17 with its closing line, every
team-game's D/ST week derived by the product's own `ds_team_defense_week` code with plan 08 §3.2 U-6
points allowed, every kicker row). **Candidates:** every team with a game that week — the D/ST is
the team; the kicker is the team's kicking unit (its K rows summed; a team that kicked nothing scores
0) — weeks 2–17 of each season (48 weeks, about 30 candidates a week). **Scorings:** the reference
league (`fx-10h`) and each probe league's own recorded `mSettings` (`league-a` standard with yardage
tiers and return yards, `league-b` half-PPR, `league-c` whose D/ST scores INT-return TDs 0 and other
return TDs 3). **Predictors:** the shipped model (`dstExpectation` / `kickerExpectation`, the E5
`kdst` slice), "last week's score" (the team's most recent game), and the implied total (D/ST: the
lowest opponent implied total; K: the highest own implied total), research 05 §8.4 #8.

Definitions, fixed before the numbers were read. **Beats "last week's score" clearly:** pooled over
the three seasons the paired weekly Spearman gain is ≥ 0.05 with its seeded bootstrap 95 % interval
(2,000 resamples of the weeks) above 0, the gain is positive in every season, and the model's MAE is
below last week's in every season. **Matches "lowest implied total":** the model orders the
candidates exactly as the implied total does, in every evaluated week.

**Result — both hold, under all four scorings, for K and D/ST (hard, green).**

- **Matches, exactly — and by construction.** The model's only matchup input is the implied total
  (`waivers.ts` passes the team's and the opponent's implied totals, nothing else), and its
  expectation is monotone in it (a fast-check property in the test), so its ranking *is* the
  baseline's: ρ model = ρ implied in all 384 scoring × position × week cells. What the model adds is
  the level — expected points under the league's own brackets — which E5's hold-or-stream margin and
  the K/D-ST surplus use. A model that out-ranks the implied total needs inputs the slice does not
  read yet (own sack and takeaway rates, the opponent's sack rate allowed — sib research 05 §8.2;
  the D5 pbp profile exists): a next step, not a C7 requirement.
- **Beats last week clearly.** D/ST: pooled ρ gain +0.275 to +0.304 by scoring, intervals from
  +0.20 to +0.37; MAE 4.4–4.75 vs 6.4–7.0. Kickers: pooled gain +0.076 to +0.102 (interval lower
  bounds +0.012 to +0.038), positive in every season, MAE 3.7–4.0 vs 5.2–5.7. Kickers clear the bar
  by less: their per-season intervals include 0 in 2023 and 2025, and the model's single top pick
  averaged *fewer* points than last week's top scorer (8.10 vs 8.61 pooled under the reference
  scoring) — one pick a week for 16 weeks is a noisy statistic, and the column is reported, not gated.
- **The D/ST level was wrong, and is fixed (`kdst.ts`).** Before this stage `rare_c` scored only
  safeties and blocks (0.20 points under every recorded scoring): the expected line carried the
  combined defensive-TD item (ESPN 94), which no recorded league scores, and no return TD at all,
  while all four score the INT- and fumble-return items (103, 104) and the kick, punt and
  blocked-kick return TDs (101, 102, 93). The realised mean of those events is 0.99 points a game
  under the reference scoring. `rare_c` now scores every TD member a realised D/ST line carries, at
  `DST_RARE_RATES` (the table below, held equal to the history by the test), and takeaways carry
  forced fumbles (106; `league-c` scores them) at `DST_FF_RATE`. The pooled D/ST level error under
  the reference scoring is now +0.04 points; no ranking changed (a constant per scoring). The MAE
  rose slightly (4.34 → 4.41 under the reference scoring) because D/ST points are right-skewed and the
  MAE rewards a median: the expectation is now mean-unbiased, which is what a hold-or-stream margin
  needs.
- **Still off, reported:** `league-a`'s return yards (114, 115) — carried since Stage C by
  `returns_e` (below; fitted on 2023, low on the held-out seasons after the NFL's kickoff-rule
  changes). Kickers are 0.73 points a game low under every scoring: the history attempts
  2.00 field goals a team-game against the model's 1.77, with more 50+ and fewer 40–49 attempts
  than `KDST.fgMix` (table below) — a proposal for the human-run `eff tune`, never applied by a test.

## C8 — news calibration

**Built** (`src/domain/evidence/calibration.ts`): the per-source calibration table and E10's
posterior over it. The retrospective keeps each claim value-free once its game is final
(`ScoredClaim`: source, claim type, signal — the claim's designation or direction —, the structured
prior it was made against and its ESPN status, the outcome; never the text); `claimOutcome` is the
labelling rule (an availability or health claim is borne out when he played as it said; a role or
coaching-intent claim when his snap share moved ≥ 10 points the way it said within two games).
`buildCalibrationTable` fits per source × type × signal × prior status (1) the reliability as a Beta
posterior around the hand-set value (10 pseudo-claims) and (2) the log-likelihood ratio the claim
carries for P(active) on top of the prior — a one-parameter logistic regression with the prior's
log-odds as offset and a N(hand-set, 1) prior (safeguarded Newton; Laplace sd). Rows are split by
status because the evidence is not additive across designations (below). `mergePActive` adds the
latest item per source; an injection-flagged item weighs 0; a prior of 0 (an official ruling) is
never moved. `evidencePosterior` is E10's gate: null until `table_n` — the scored **news** claims
(RSS, ESPN outlooks, user claims) — reaches 200 (sib ADV OBJ-21), then the posterior shown and the
"priors are hand-set" note dropped. The official practice report is a calibration source of its own,
seeded from history (`PRACTICE_REPORT_HISTORY`, `practiceHistoryClaims()`), never counted in `table_n`.

**Measured on the labelled history that exists** (`tests/domain/evidence/calibration-history.test.ts`;
data `tests/domain/evidence/data/injury_outcomes`: the nflverse injury report 2023–2025 joined to snap
counts, 16,772 of 17,188 rows labelled; outcome = took a snap). Designation-only is the shipped
`P_ACTIVE_BY_STATUS` (no designation = 1, Questionable 0.71, Doubtful 0.059, Out 0); "re-fitted"
replaces those base rates with the training seasons' rates; "E10 evidence-augmented" is the shipped
prior merged with E10's official practice-report item through a table fitted on the training seasons
only. Held out by season.

**Reading.** E10's evidence-augmented P(active) beats designation-only on both held-out seasons —
by 0.0041 and 0.0038 Brier over all positions, 0.0062 and 0.0056 over QB/RB/WR/TE/K, every interval
below 0 — and beats the re-fitted base rates too, so the gain is the evidence's, not only a
recalibration. Where it comes from (pooled table below): a player with no game designation who did
not practise by Friday played 80 % of the time (designation-only says 100 %); a Questionable player
who did not practise, 53 %; one who practised fully, 73 % (designation-only says 71 % for both).
The shipped base rates themselves run high on this history — Questionable 0.67 observed vs 0.71,
Doubtful 0.01 vs 0.059, a listed player with no designation 0.94 vs 1 — a recalibration for
`P_ACTIVE_BY_STATUS`'s owner to weigh (not changed here).

**What C8 still needs — time (needs_time, the code path proven).** `calibration_state.table_n ≥ 200`
counts scored news claims, and no news claim has labelled history: B8's 71 labelled RSS items are
2026 week-5 claims captured before their games, and ESPN's finished-season box scores carry no
injury status or outlook. So `table_n` is 0 today, E10 keeps `posterior: null` with "priors are
hand-set", and `news-check` keeps the line, until the retrospective has scored 200 claims in season;
`calibration.test.ts` proves the switch (199 → null and the note; 200 → the posterior, the note
gone) and that the official history alone never flips it. The RSS-text half of "news-augmented
Brier ≤ designation-only" is measured the same way once those claims exist. Wiring the table into
E10 (and the retrospective's claim scoring) is outside this stage's paths and is listed for its
